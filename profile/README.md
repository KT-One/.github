# KT One

> _Science in vein._

KT One is an independent research lab founded to accelerate human understanding at the intersection of quantum computing, quantum machine learning, and artificial intelligence.

We operate as a research-first organization - small, focused, and deliberately early-stage. Our work sits at the frontier of open quantum systems and quantum AI, with an emphasis on the physics that actually governs near-term hardware.

## Why This Matters

Current NISQ hardware is inherently noisy and dissipative. Most quantum ML research treats this as a liability. We treat it as the material.

Collision models and non-Markovian dynamics give us a precise language for what noise actually _does_ - and how it can be shaped into a computational resource rather than eliminated. Dissipative QNN is one of the few globally active research threads working from this direction.

## Lab Structure

KT One runs a hybrid human-AI R&D pipeline. We are an AI-first organization, built on the belief that AI will free human researchers from routine tasks and enable deeper focus on creative, high-level scientific work. Research is organized around parallel hypothesis exploration, with structured coordination between human researchers and AI agents for literature review, theoretical development, and implementation.

We are pre-funding and pre-product - entirely focused on research output quality and foundational positioning.
